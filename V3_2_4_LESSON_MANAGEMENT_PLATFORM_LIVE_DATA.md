# v3.2.4 Lesson Management & Platform Live Data

- Tutors can delete lessons they own/manage.
- Tutors can rename lessons directly from Lesson Manager.
- Tutors can move a lesson from one module to another while preserving course/programme metadata and assigning the next target-module order.
- Platform dashboard now loads trusted server-side live counts for users, students, tutors, tenants, plans, subscriptions, programmes, course units, modules, lessons, quizzes, marketplace products, fulfilled orders, revenue, support and AI usage.
- Platform Recent Activity now uses recorded commerce, notification and AI events.
- Subscription Plans now loads created plans through a trusted platform-admin callable so saved plans display even when browser Firestore rules block direct plan reads.
