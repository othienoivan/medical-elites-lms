# V1 Clinical Logbook and Competency Upgrade

## Added
- Structured encounter type and participation level.
- Clinical placement/rotation and verified clinical hours.
- Learning-outcome mapping and anonymized evidence links.
- Supervisor competency rating: communication, clinical reasoning, professionalism and procedural skill.
- Overall competency level from needs improvement to proficient.
- Student dashboard totals for approved clinical hours and competent/proficient entries.
- Firestore student-update allowlist expanded for the new student-owned fields.

## Privacy
Students are explicitly instructed not to record patient names, contacts, addresses, hospital numbers, faces or other identifiers.
